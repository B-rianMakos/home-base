Home Base

A free Windows app that keeps one family's events, tasks and reminders in one place. It runs on the family's own PC at home and is used only by that family.

What it does with Google

When a family member adds an event, the app creates it in the Google Calendar of the family's own Google account (the family's admin account, or a second family account), and Google sends the invitations. The app asks Google for one permission only: to view and edit calendar events. It doesn't read email, contacts or files.

Where the data is

Everything stays on the family's PC. There is no server on the internet, no account to create, no advertising and no tracking.

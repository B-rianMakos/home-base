Privacy policy

Home Base · last updated October 29, 2026

Who this is for

Home Base is a free app that each family runs for itself, on its own PC at home. This policy covers a family's own copy: there is no shared service, and the authors never see any family's data.

What the app accesses

  • Google Calendar events only. With the Google permission "View and edit events on all your calendars" (calendar.events), the app creates, changes and cancels the family's own events, and reads who has accepted or declined them. It doesn't read email, contacts, files or any other Google data.
  • The Google account's email address, so the app can check that the right family account was connected.

Where data is kept

All the app's data (events, tasks, reminders, the family's email addresses, and the Google sign-in tokens) is stored on the family's own PC at home. The sign-in tokens are encrypted. Nothing is sent to any server run by the app's authors, because there isn't one. Data goes to Google only to create, change or cancel calendar events and to send invitations, and to Gmail only when a family member asks for their daily summary email.

Sharing

The app doesn't sell, share or transfer data to anyone, and doesn't use it for advertising or for training AI or machine-learning models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A connected account can be disconnected at any time in the app (Family & Settings → Calendar accounts → Disconnect), which also revokes the app's access at Google. Access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
